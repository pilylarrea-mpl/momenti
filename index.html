The House

A moment, made tangible.

Momenti makes collectible objects from moments in time. A photograph is printed on a frame clutch, and one detail from it is sculpted in miniature for the clasp.

A street in Havana in 1958, or the afternoon of a wedding. Any moment worth keeping.

The signature

The clasp.

It is always taken from the photograph itself. The car parked at the curb. The cab waiting in the rain. Look at the print, then at the top of the piece.

Each clasp is sculpted and cast in metal, then painted by hand to match the print. It is the house signature.

    Client Services

    Write to . We reply within one working day.

    How do pre-orders work?

    Send the form. Nothing is charged. Your number, delivery window and payment are confirmed by email before production.

    What is the first batch?

    The first pieces made in each edition. Where it is open, a $100 deposit holds your place and is deducted from the price. Once full, pre-orders move to the second batch.

    Can I get my deposit back?

    Yes, in full, until production begins. We write before it does.

    When will my piece arrive?

    Each piece is made to order. Your delivery window is confirmed with your pre-order, and again at dispatch.

    Where do you ship?

    Worldwide, tracked and insured. Duties are shown before payment.

    Can I choose my edition number?

    Request one between 1 and 25. If it is free, it is yours.

    How should I care for my piece?

    Store in its dust bag, away from sun and damp. Wipe the frame with a soft, dry cloth. Hold the clutch by its body, not the clasp.
