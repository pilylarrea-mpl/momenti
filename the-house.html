Our story

Moments are the only luxury you can’t buy.

From Pily

I named MOMENTI after my grandparents’ story.

They had to leave Cuba. The moments they lived there became part of history, and part of us, and I wanted a way to carry them with me.

So the first bag I made is Havana, 1958: a street in Havana printed on the bag, and on the clasp a small turquoise car from that street.

“So much hurt and pain behind it, but also memories. I felt proud.”

Pily

MOMENTI

A design house that turns real moments into bags you carry. Each one begins with a single photograph of a place, in a year, and keeps its feeling.

See Havana, 1958

From a moment to a piece.How each bag is made

The moment.

Every bag begins with one moment: one place, in one year. It is printed edge to edge, front and back.

The car.

One detail from it, a car from that street, is sculpted in miniature and painted to match. It sits on the clasp, and lifting it opens the bag.

The story.

Each bag comes with a card telling the true story behind its moment, with a line for your name.
