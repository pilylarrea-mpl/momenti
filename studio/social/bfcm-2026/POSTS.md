## 1. "Up to 60% off." I counted. (`audit/`)

The fact: across five stores and 2,074 in-stock pieces, the headline discount sits on a small share. Heaven Mayhem 25 of 180 at 60%. Jet Set Candy 58 of 923 at 50%. alice + olivia 10 of 713 at 60%. Alexandra Miro goes the other way: 63 of 125 are half off or better.

Enemy: the words "up to". Never the brands. Every slide ends on a piece that really is at the top discount, so the post helps the brand sell.

Hook options for the first line of the caption or a voiceover:
1. "Up to 60% off." I counted how many pieces actually are.
2. I opened the full catalog of five Black Friday sales and counted.
3. 25 of 180. That's how many pieces are really 60% off.
4. One brand said 20 to 50% off. Half the store was 50% or more.
5. I'm reading 376 Black Friday sales so you can skip 370 of them.

Caption: "Up to" is doing a lot of work this week. I went through five stores piece by piece. The real 60% is on slide 2. Save this before you check out. (Commissionable links in bio.)

Goal: saves. Pin a comment asking which store to count next, and count it. That's tomorrow's post.

## 2 to 6. The Grand Tour (`tour-1` to `tour-5`)

The sort: Black Friday by city, one Momenti bag per post, every pick matched to that bag's colours and year. Nobody else can run this sort, and the bag appears on every slide without being the thing for sale.

Hook options for N° 1:
1. I sorted Black Friday by city. First stop: Havana, 1958. (on the cover now)
2. Black Friday is 376 sales. I kept the five things that belong in Havana.
3. Everyone sorts Black Friday by percent off. I sorted it by where you'd wear it.
4. If your outfit had a passport stamp: Havana, 1958.
5. Five things on sale this week that look like 1958.

Captions (swap the city):
- Havana: Stop 1 of 5. Red satin, clip-on earrings, a silver domino. Everything here is on sale through Dec 1. Monaco is next. (Commissionable links in bio.)
- Monaco: Stop 2. A $980 choker for $384 and a poker chip for $74. New York tomorrow.
- New York: Stop 3. Pinstripes, sequins and a $22 JFK tag.
- Miami: Stop 4. A $98 swimsuit and the only sunglasses sale of the year.
- Tokyo: Last stop. Which city are you? Tell me and I'll build yours.

Goal: follows on 1 to 4 (the last slide names the next city), comments on 5.

## 7. Six sale finds that fit in a clutch (`clutch/`)

The sort: by size. All six fit in an evening bag, from a $13 lipstick up. The last slide totals the bag: $802 down to $440.25. Its closing line is the one place the series says Momenti makes the bag.

Hook options:

## The video

`tour-1-havana-video-silent.mp4` is a 15-second cut of the Havana slides with a slow push-in. It has no sound: add a trending audio in TikTok.

Three better videos, in the order I'd make them:

1. **Green screen, you talking, the spreadsheet behind you.** This is the audit post. Screen-record yourself scrolling the 376-row sheet, then the Heaven Mayhem sale page sorted by price. Say the count out loud. Yap for four minutes, cut the best 40 seconds. Your playbook rates this format highest and it costs nothing.
2. **Real brand footage, one brand per video.** Three stores have campaign clips on their own homepages: Heaven Mayhem (three 10-second clips of the watch and earrings on a model), Alexandra Miro (an 8-second street walk in the red print) and Margaux (20 seconds of the shoes being made by hand). Price and percent on screen, nothing else. Ask each brand through ShopMy before posting their footage; most say yes to affiliates, and the ask is itself a relationship.
3. **Your hands, your bag.** Film the six clutch items going into a real clutch on a table, one cut per item. This one needs you to own the items, so it waits for a later sale.

Don't cut the Momenti bag clips into brand footage. The bag clips are generated, the brand clips are real, and your own rule is not to mix them.
