## How every item was chosen

For 20 of the 21 brands I downloaded the store's whole catalog, ranked in-stock items by real markdown, and looked at the product photos before choosing. Each item is in stock and either 30% off or more, or from a brand that rarely discounts. Brands where I couldn't find at least three items that passed were cut. That is why home and makeup have four brands each.

The exception is Jenni Kayne: the catalog confirms each coat's 50% or 60% sale tag, and the sale prices are that percentage applied to the listed price.

## Recording the voice

Upload the video, tap Voiceover in TikTok's editor, and talk while it plays. Each brand stays up about 4.7 seconds, which is roughly twelve words. The notes below are facts to say in your own words. The screen already shows the brand and the discount, so use your voice for which piece you'd buy.

Open each the same way: "Black Friday, [travel] edition. I read 376 sales so you don't have to." Close with the next edition's name.
