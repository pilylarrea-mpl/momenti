The House

Objects from real moments.

Each piece starts from a photograph of a real place and year. The print is made from it. One detail is sculpted as the clasp. On Collection I it is a car, and it opens.

A street in Havana in 1958, or the afternoon of a wedding. Any moment worth keeping.

The signature

The clasp.

It is always taken from the photograph itself. The car parked at the curb. The cab waiting in the rain. Look at the print, then at the top of the piece.

Each clasp is sculpted and cast in metal, then painted by hand to match the print. It is the house signature.

    How does the waitlist work?

    Choose a piece and join its waitlist by saving a card with Stripe. Nothing is charged today. When your piece is made, we write at least a week before charging your card, with your delivery window.

    Can I cancel?

    Yes, any time before your card is charged. Reply to any email from us. If a run is not made, your card is never charged.

    When will my piece arrive?

    Each piece is made to order. Your delivery window is confirmed by email before your card is charged, and again at dispatch.

    Where do you ship?

    Worldwide, tracked and insured. Duties are shown before payment.

    How should I care for my piece?

    Store in its dust bag, away from sun and damp. Wipe the frame with a soft, dry cloth. Hold the clutch by its body, not the clasp.
