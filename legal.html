Reservation terms.

Last updated 18 November 2026.

What a reservation is

A reservation saves your place for a bag that is sold out. Your card is saved securely by Stripe, and nothing is charged when you reserve.

When you are charged

When your bag is ready, we email you. You have seven days from that email to change your mind. If you keep it, the price shown when you reserved is charged to the card you saved, and your bag is sent.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time before the charge, by replying to any email from us or writing to hello@momenti.com. Nothing is charged.

Your card

Payments are handled securely by our payment provider, Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

Terms of sale.

Prices

Prices are shown in US dollars. Shipping, and any duties or taxes for your country, are shown before you pay.

Questions

Write to hello@momenti.com. A person reads every email.

Shipping.

When

Each bag is made after it’s reserved. You’ll receive an email with tracking on the day it’s sent.

How

Tracked and insured, with a signature on delivery. The parcel is plain: nothing on the outside says what’s inside.

Duties and taxes

Shown to you before you are charged.

If it’s late

We’ll tell you before you have to ask.

Returns.

If it isn’t right

Write to hello@momenti.com with your order number, and we’ll take care of it.

If it arrived damaged

That isn’t a return. Write to hello@momenti.com with photos, and we’ll put it right.

Care.

The car

It’s small, and it opens the bag. Lift it with a fingertip, not a nail, and never carry the bag by it.

The frame

Wipe it with a soft, dry cloth. Close it gently: the frame clicks shut by itself.

The print

Keep perfume, hand cream and sunscreen off it until they’ve dried on your skin. Keep it out of strong sun for long stretches.

Storing it

In its dust bag, lying flat with the car facing up. Never in plastic, and never in a hot car in summer.

Privacy.

What we keep

Your email if you join the House, the moment you send if you nominate one, and your name, address and email if you reserve. Card details stay with Stripe.

Cookies

Where the law asks us to, we ask before using any cookies, and “No thanks” is always as easy as “Accept”.

Leaving the list

Every letter has a link to leave, in one click.

The Letter

Letters from the house: new moments, first.

You’re on the list.
